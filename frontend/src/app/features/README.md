# Features Module

## Overview
Domain feature modules implementing business capabilities for the pizzeria management platform, encompassing AI assistance, order management, kitchen operations, inventory, recipe costing, pricing intelligence, and public storefronts.

## Subdirectories & Boundaries
- AI Assistant: [./ai/README.md](./ai/README.md) - Conversational business intelligence, natural language queries, and dynamic analytical chart generation.
- Operational Alerts: [./alerts/README.md](./alerts/README.md) - Real-time operational notifications, stock thresholds, and margin alerts.
- Executive Dashboard: [./dashboard/README.md](./dashboard/README.md) - High-level business KPIs, sales rankings, and revenue charts.
- Operating Fees: [./fees/README.md](./fees/README.md) - Payment gateway fees, delivery splits, and public store slug settings.
- Fixed Costs: [./fixed-costs/README.md](./fixed-costs/README.md) - Monthly operational overhead management incorporated into pricing formulas.
- Raw Ingredients: [./ingredients/README.md](./ingredients/README.md) - Raw ingredient inventory tracking, unit costs, and stock replenishment.
- Kitchen Display System: [./kitchen/README.md](./kitchen/README.md) - Production order queue, recipe expansion, elapsed timers, and sound alerts.
- Delivery Neighborhoods: [./neighborhoods/README.md](./neighborhoods/README.md) - Delivery zone coverage, delivery fee schedules, and estimated transit times.
- Order Management: [./orders/README.md](./orders/README.md) - In-store POS order creation, multi-flavor pizza assembly, and thermal ticket printing.
- Recipe Preparations: [./preparations/README.md](./preparations/README.md) - Semi-finished intermediate preparations and batch yield calculations.
- Pricing Intelligence: [./pricing/README.md](./pricing/README.md) - Multi-flavor split pricing, combo bundle margins, and beverage markups.
- Product Catalog: [./products/README.md](./products/README.md) - Menu products, categories, pizza sizes, and slice configurations.
- Profit Reporting: [./profit/README.md](./profit/README.md) - DRE financial statements, gross margin breakdowns, and channel profitability.
- Public Storefront: [./public/README.md](./public/README.md) - Customer-facing digital menu, shopping cart checkout, and live order tracking.
- Stock Ledger: [./stock/README.md](./stock/README.md) - Inventory audit ledger, movement transactions, and AI replenishment urgency reports.
- Team Administration: [./team/README.md](./team/README.md) - Employee user accounts, role-based permissions, and credentials management.
- Technical Sheets: [./technical-sheets/README.md](./technical-sheets/README.md) - Direct cost of goods sold (CMV) engineering and portion scaling.
- WhatsApp Omnichannel: [./whatsapp/README.md](./whatsapp/README.md) - QR code instance pairing, customer chat threads, and AI order drafting.
